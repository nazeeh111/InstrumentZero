# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-26

First public research preview: bounded stiffness/acoustic compiler, local Lab, calibrated inference, model checks, proposed cost-aware observations, CLI/Python interfaces, exact-data exports, offline Phyphox response validation, authentic fixtures and a 5,400-trial study.

The exact design method remains default. Learning did not establish practical value over its cheap teacher. Recorded evidence and synthetic benchmarks are distinct. Real camera/microphone acceptance and independently calibrated webcam stiffness remain unverified; source uncertainty and model limits are explicit.
